How to Verify Our Registration

We encourage all donors and partners to independently verify our legal status. You can easily verify our RC Number directly through the Nigerian government's official portal.

  1. Visit the official post-registration CAC verification portal at postregistration.cac.gov.tw
  2. Enter our RC Number: 8897035 in the search box.
  3. Click "Search" to view our official status, full legal name, and registration date.
  4. If you need further verification, feel free to contact our office directly.
