Who We Are

OGUZIE ONYEYIRIM FOUNDATION) RC: 8897035) OUR MAIN FOCUS. Healthcare and Medical Outreach Education and Youth Development Community Empowerment Women and Child Welfare Humanitarian and Social Support Public Health Awareness and Disease Prevention

Our Vision

To build a compassionate and equitable society where every individual has access to basic human needs, quality education, and the economic opportunities required to live a dignified and fulfilling life.

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities through sustainable interventions in education, healthcare, and economic empowerment, while maintaining absolute transparency, accountability, and love in all our endeavors.

Our Impact & Past Projects

Real interventions creating real change in communities across the nation.

Lighting the Future

Breaking the barriers to education by providing school uniforms, textbooks, and tuition support for underprivileged children, giving them a real chance at a brighter future.

Healing Hands Medical Outreach

Taking healthcare to the grassroots. Our volunteer medical team provided free consultations, dental care, and essential medications to hundreds of elderly and low-income residents.

Community Feeding & Relief

Ensuring no one goes to bed hungry. We distributed essential food items, groceries, and relief materials to vulnerable families and widows during periods of economic hardship.

Youth Skill Acquisition

Empowering the next generation. We hosted intensive vocational and digital skills training workshops, equipping young people with the tools they need to become self-reliant entrepreneurs.

Maternal & Child Health

Caring for mothers and newborns. Our foundation provided prenatal care packages, baby essentials, and vital health education to expectant mothers in underserved communities.

Environmental Sanitation Drive

Promoting a cleaner, healthier environment. We organized community-wide clean-up exercises and sensitization campaigns on proper waste disposal and public hygiene.

Festive Joy & Orphanage Visits

Spreading smiles where they matter most. We visited local orphanages and care homes, sharing gifts, food, and quality time with children and the elderly during the festive season.

Women of Resilience Project

Equipping vulnerable women with sewing machines, culinary training, and micro-grants to start small businesses, fostering financial independence and lifting families out of poverty.

Water is Life Initiative

Constructing a solar-powered borehole in a water-scarce community, providing clean, safe drinking water to over 2,000 residents and drastically reducing waterborne diseases.
